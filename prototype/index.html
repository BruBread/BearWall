    Devices

    Phase 2 preview: pretend a device joined your Wi-Fi and see how the wall changes. Widgets follow their "Show when" setting.
    Test alerts
    These are simulated. Later the projector finds devices itself: it pings every address on your network, reads its neighbor table, looks up each brand from the hardware address, and reads the names devices announce (mDNS). Phones use a random hardware address on each network, so you register yours once. The PC agent reports music, mic and camera.
